The hidden operating system
behind 36 startup ideas.

A list that looks like brainstorming is actually one tight thesis applied 36 times. Here's the framework underneath — the master forces, the 8 buckets they generate, and how he likely produced the list in the first place.

AI compresses the cost of doing things. That destroys old business models, frees up human time, and starves us of connection. Every opportunity on the list is either riding the compression, selling the picks & shovels, or selling the antidote.

How he generated the list — the 6 lenses

1. Demographic deltas

Supply shrinking or demand swelling that nobody priced in. Boomers aging, trades retiring, loneliness rising.

2. AI-applied-to-X scanning

Walk every industry. Ask: what does an agent do here that a SaaS tool can't?

3. Business-model flips

One vertical changed pricing/packaging. Who else hasn't followed? (Lawn → pest, seats → outcomes.)

4. Counter-trend reactions

For every dominant trend, what's the backlash market? AI slop → human verification, analog, anti-screen.

5. Underserved-yet-massive

$100B+ industries with embarrassingly little tech. Pets, small farms, family logistics.

6. Psychological needs

Maslow's middle layers are the most monetizable right now: belonging, meaning, mastery.

The 3 master forces — everything reduces to these

Force A

AI is eating work

Agents replace job functions and the infrastructure to operate them appears underneath.

Force B

AI is creating a vacuum of meaning & humanness

As machines do more, demand for connection, embodiment, and authenticity spikes.

Force C

Old models break before new ones settle

Pricing, packaging, and demographics shift faster than incumbents can.
